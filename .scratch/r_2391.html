Frequently asked questions

Fundamental Data
  • Major US Companies: from 1985 (30+ years)
  • Non-US Companies: from 2000 (21+ years)
  • Minor Companies: last 6 years

Intraday Data
  • US (NYSE & NASDAQ):
    • 1-minute intervals: from 2004 (includes premarket & afterhours)
    • 5-minute & 1-hour intervals: from October 2020
  • FOREX & Cryptocurrencies:
    • 1-minute intervals: from 2009
    • 5-minute & 1-hour intervals: from October 2020
  • Other Markets:
    • 5-minute & 1-hour intervals: from October 2020
    • 1-minute data: available for some tickers from October 2020 (varies by exchange)

End-of-Day (EOD) & Dividends Data
  • Global coverage: 150,000+ tickers worldwide
  • US Stocks, ETFs, Mutual Funds: from earliest available (e.g., Ford Motors from Jun 1972)
  • Non-US Exchanges: mostly from Jan 3, 2000

Tick Data (US Only)
  • Start Date: January 1st, 2008
