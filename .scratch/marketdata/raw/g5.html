Our Data Sources and Data Partners

Via MCP server Via Coding Libraries & SDK

For end-of-day (EOD) and delayed data, we have direct contracts with several exchanges for their corresponding data streams. We get USA data from Nasdaq Cloud API. We get European data from Cboe Europe Equities (Cboe) covering Western and Eastern European markets. For Australian feeds, we have a direct agreement with the Australian Stock Exchange (ASX). Delayed and End of Day NEO Exchange Data for NEO and TSX/TSXV powered by Quotemedia.com. All rights reserved. View the Terms of Use. Data is delayed 15 minutes unless indicated.

It’s not just words and logos as many other sources do. We can provide proof for all mentioned above contracts for our subscribers by request.

Another end-of-day (EOD) and delayed (LIVE) API data comes from CFDs and market makers.

For fundamental data, we collect data from announcements, financial news providers, investor relations sections of corporate websites, and from published annual reports. For example, for the USA markets, we collect data directly from reports published on sec.gov, for Canada – directly from sedar.com. And then we re-compile and re-calculate the data on our side. In most cases for fast and proven calculations, we use Altova solutions, like Altova RaptorXML (link). We also use Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLM) and Machine Learning (ML) to read and interpret data from company news, press releases, financial reports, declarations, and announcements.

All fundamental data and fields are collected and standardized on our side. The data for companies with large and mid capitalizations for major exchanges we re-check manually by our team.
