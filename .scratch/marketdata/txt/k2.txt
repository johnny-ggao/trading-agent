What are pro and non-pro classifications for Massive's stock data? Stocks Business FAQ Massive asks one set of eleven registration questions for every asset class: answer yes to question one and no to the other ten and you are non-professional. Individual plans are for non-professional use; professional use needs a Business plan. There is no stock-specific version of this test, which is the first thing worth knowing: Massive asks the same eleven questions whether the subscription you want is Stocks, Options, Indices, Currencies or Futures. The exchange definitions sitting behind them are not identical, so if you land near the line, read the one covering the data you want. Work through them on Am I a professional or non-professional subscriber? , then come back here for what it means for a Stocks subscription. Applies to Plans: Stocks Basic, Starter, Developer and Advanced are Individual plans and are for non-professional subscribers. Stocks Business carries the commercial licence. Endpoints: all of them. Nothing in the API behaves differently, because this is a licensing question rather than a technical one. Asset classes: US stocks and ETFs. Massive asks the same eleven questions for every other asset class too, though the exchange definitions behind them differ. Who sets the rule for stocks For US stocks the definitions come from the exchanges behind the consolidated tape, the CTA and UTP plans, and from the FINRA facilities that carry off-exchange prints. Their fee schedules charge per user and per category, and they hold the vendor responsible for classifying users correctly. Massive applies their definitions rather than writing its own, which is why the answer does not change if you move to another vendor. What it decides for a Stocks subscription Your classification decides which side of the catalogue you can buy from, and that is a bigger difference than a price: What changes Non-professional Professional Plans you can buy Stocks Basic, Starter, Developer, Advanced Stocks Business Where to buy massive.com/pricing , self-serve massive.com/business , some self-serve, the rest through sales What you may do with the data Your own personal, non-business use Whatever the commercial licence covers, including showing it to clients Redistribution Not permitted Needs a licence that covers it explicitly Do not assume the Business plan is the Individual plan at a higher price. The Business plans are packaged differently, and some data an Individual Advanced tier includes is sold as a separate expansion on the Business side. How to get it right Take the eleven-question test on Am I a professional or non-professional subscriber? . If you sit near the line, describe what you are doing in a chat at massive.com/contact and we will tell you which side you fall on. If you are professional and want help choosing, go to sales through massive.com/business or sales@massive.com . If your circumstances change, tell us. Correcting it forward is routine; discovering it in an audit is not. Why the exchanges care The exchanges care because their fee schedules charge per user and per category, so the count and the classification of users is what they are paid on. That is why the question is asked at signup rather than left to you, and why the difference in price is a licensing cost rather than a difference in the quality of the data. Citizenship and location are not part of the test: use is. If you see an error Nothing in the API refuses a request because of your classification, so a refusal is about your subscription rather than your status. {"status":"NOT_AUTHORIZED"} means the product or tier does not cover the request. It is not a professional-status check. A Stocks plan that will not let you buy it is usually an account already marked professional. That is a classification answer, not a billing fault. Data Coverage Category Data Coverage Stocks Category Stocks Description Yes. Off-exchange prints, which include dark pool executions, arrive on the ordinary trades feed and are identified by an exchange ID of 4. There is no separate dark pool endpoint, and no way to tell which individual venue a print came from. Title Does Massive offer pre-market and after-hours data? FAQ Category FAQ Stocks Category Stocks Description Yes. Every extended-hours trade and quote is published, from 04:00 to 20:00 Eastern. There is no session parameter, so you select a session with timestamps, and expect far fewer aggregate bars outside regular hours because most extended-hours trades do not update a bar.
